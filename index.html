01 / About us

The celebration of happiness, resilience, and authenticity found in queer life.

The Queer Research Network is an emerging initiative at the University of Calgary that brings together 2SLGBTQIA+ students, staff, faculty, and allies interested in research, collaboration, and community.

The network centres queer experiences across disciplines while creating space to share ideas, highlight queer scholarship, foster collaboration, and build visibility for queer research.

02 / Queer research

The Gay Agenda (of Research)

Queer and trans communities have long been the subjects of research, but the experiences and priorities have not always shaped the questions researchers ask (Marshall et al., 2019; Everhart et al., 2022). For us, queer research means being more intentional about whose questions we prioritize, whose knowledge we value, and how the work we support can better reflect the lives, strengths, and joy of queer communities (Everhart et al., 2022; Pang et al., 2026; Callaghan & van Leent, 2019). We want this space to celebrate that work and the people behind it!
